About the Company

Serving the UAE's workforce needs since 2012

Shabab Deira is a UAE-based manpower, labour, logistics, and workforce services company — reliable workers and support services for businesses across the Emirates.

Book Manpower Contact Operations

From Deira, for the whole UAE

Shabab Deira was founded in 2012 by Zubair Khan in Deira, Dubai — steps from the creek, the port, and the trading heart of the city. The company began by serving the businesses around it: cargo handlers, traders, and shopkeepers who needed dependable hands.

Fourteen years on, that same principle guides a much wider operation. Today Shabab Deira supplies workforce services across all seven emirates — packing, loading, delivery, cleaning, catering, events, machine operation, driving, port work, and general support — to logistics, retail, manufacturing, events, and facilities clients.

The company is part of a group of five specialised UAE companies, each focused on one part of the cargo and workforce chain. Clients deal with one point of contact — and draw on the capabilities of the whole group.
