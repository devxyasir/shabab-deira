About the Company

Serving the UAE's workforce needs since 2012

Shabab Deira is a UAE-based manpower, labour, logistics, and workforce services company — reliable workers and support services for businesses across the Emirates.

From Deira, for the whole UAE

Shabab Deira was founded in 2012 by Zubair Khan in Deira, Dubai — steps from the creek, the port, and the trading heart of the city. The company began by serving the businesses around it: cargo handlers, traders, and shopkeepers who needed dependable hands.

Fourteen years on, that same principle guides a much wider operation. Today Shabab Deira supplies workforce services across all seven emirates — packing, loading, delivery, cleaning, catering, events, machine operation, driving, port work, and general support — to logistics, retail, manufacturing, events, and facilities clients.

The company is part of a group of five specialised UAE companies, each focused on one part of the cargo and workforce chain. Clients deal with one point of contact — and draw on the capabilities of the whole group.

Our Approach

How we operate

Worker screening

Every candidate is verified for experience, valid documentation, and medical fitness before joining any assignment.

Legal compliance

Workers are employed under valid UAE residency, medically insured, and paid on time through the Wage Protection System (WPS).

Operational support

A direct line to our operations desk in Deira — supervision, replacements, and answers when you need them.

What We Stand For

Principles that guide our operations

Honesty in scope

We supply only the services we can genuinely deliver. If a requirement falls outside what we do, we say so clearly.

Worker welfare

Fair wages, paid on time, and proper treatment. Compliance is not just regulatory — it is how we keep a reliable workforce.

Responsive operations

When you contact us, you speak to someone who can act — our operations team in Deira handles requirements directly.

Long-term reliability

We aim to be a trusted regular supplier, not a one-time vendor. Consistent quality builds that relationship.

Part of a wider group

Shabab Deira is one of five specialised companies in the group — covering container loading, cargo packaging, contract labour, technical services, and shipping. Learn more on Our Group.
